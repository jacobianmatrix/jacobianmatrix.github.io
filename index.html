Medical robotics is a complex and diverse system built upon the convergence of engineering and medicine. It encompasses a broad spectrum of categories, ranging from surgical robots that support precise diagnosis and minimally invasive interventions to rehabilitation and autonomous mobility devices designed for patient recovery. With the recent integration of artificial intelligence and high-precision control, these technologies are establishing themselves as essential medical mechanisms that overcome clinical limitations and enhance the quality of healthcare. The significance of these advancements lies in their ability to maximize patient safety and innovatively improve surgical accuracy for clinicians.

This space introduces the research of Dr. Youngjin Moon (문영진), a participating faculty member of the Medical Robotics Research Team at Asan Medical Center and University of Ulsan College of Medicine.
